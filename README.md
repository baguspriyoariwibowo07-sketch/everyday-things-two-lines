A two-line entry, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The entry

> An escalator is a staircase that got tired of standing still, so now it walks upstairs for you while you just stand there like a king on a moving carpet.
> The carpet works because both carry you up without walking, but it leaves out that stairs wait when you stop and an escalator never does.

## Why these two lines

- **A thing the field left alone:** the 569 entries crowd onto toasters, traffic jams, queues and rain. The escalator is an everyday machine a 10-year-old rides weekly, and nobody in the field explains it.
- **The analogy does the teaching:** a staircase that got tired of standing still gives the child the machine's whole idea in one picture — steps that move so you do not have to — and the king on the moving carpet makes the standing-still part feel true.
- **Line 2 is honest and specific:** it names what the carpet maps (being carried up without walking) and the one real thing it leaves out — stairs wait when you stop; an escalator never does. That is a mechanical difference, not a decorative limit.
- **Inside the gate with room:** 291 characters against the 400-character gate, exactly two lines.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
